What is Databricks and How is it Useful for Edmunds? Databricks is a cloud-based, fully managed, big data and analytics processing platform that leverages Apache Spark and the JVM. The big selling point of the Databricks Unified Analytics Platform is that it unifies Big Data and Machine Learning.
